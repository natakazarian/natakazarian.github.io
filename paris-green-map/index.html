Interpretation

The Paris green-space paradox

01

Proximity is high

Nearly the entire city lies within 500 m of a mapped green or assimilated feature.

02

Quantity is low

The analysis reports a median of 0.68 m² per resident, well below the cited planning benchmark.

03

No clear luxury effect

The income gradient is visible, but green-space provision does not reproduce it clearly at IRIS level.

Method and limitations. The buffer uses Euclidean distance rather than pedestrian routes. Mapped features have different levels of public accessibility, and the per-capita indicator does not capture cross-boundary park use. No formal correlation test was conducted. *Summary figures follow the accompanying analysis; the map uses the supplied GeoJSON export.
